Micah Reich

I'm an aerospace GNC engineer at a stealth startup in San Francisco. I recently completed my M.S. in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University, where I was advised by Zac Manchester in the Robotic Exploration Lab. My work focuses on numerical optimization for robotics and aerospace: trajectory optimization, contact-implicit planning, and guidance, navigation, and control.

Before that, I received my B.S. in Computer Science with a concentration in robotics from CMU's School of Computer Science. Previously, I have interned on GNC teams at SpaceX, Zipline, and Anduril.
